Research project

Genova VR

A generative AI framework that transforms educational prompts into editable virtual reality experiences.

Peer review pending

Project overview

Genova VR connects experience planning, scene composition, external object modeling, narrated explanations, quizzes, selective revision, and publication for VR playback.

  1. Educational prompt
  2. Planning and generation
  3. Author review and revision
  4. Published VR experience

The engineering case study examines solar-system formation, blood circulation, and mechanical-clock operation. It analyzes generated artifacts and documented playback observations, not learning effectiveness.

Manuscript

Genova VR: A Generative AI Framework for Transforming Prompts into Complete Educational Virtual Reality Experiences

Jonathan da Silva Santos and Jean Pierre Raulin
Universidade Presbiteriana Mackenzie, Brazil

The manuscript has been submitted to IEEE Access and awaits peer review. It has not been accepted. This page provides a brief project summary, not the full manuscript.

Code, data, and reproducibility

Upon article acceptance, the complete original Genova VR source code and essential resources needed to reproduce the study and corroborate its reported results will be released through this repository.

These materials include experience descriptions, case inventories, screenshots, memory observations, hardware metadata, and reproduction scripts and instructions. They are not publicly released here yet.

Before acceptance, code, data, and essential reproducibility materials will also be provided to IEEE Access editors upon request through the corresponding author.

The original source code will be released under the MIT License. Third-party components and assets retain their respective licenses. Credentials, private operational records, and other unpublished manuscripts will not be distributed.
